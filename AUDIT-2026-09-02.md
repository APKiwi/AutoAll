# Auto All (Fixed) code audit, 2026-09-02

Eight module-family reviews over 16,205 lines of Lua, each briefed with the ten
bug classes this codebase had already taught us. Every high and medium finding
below, and a sample of lows, was then verified against the source by reading
the cited lines. 86 unique findings. None of the verified ones were wrong.

The Medicine/Reload reviewer re-verified its own uncertain items against the
vanilla Lua under `Project Zomboid.app/Contents/Java/media/lua` before
reporting. Where a finding cites vanilla behaviour, that is why.

Paths are relative to `Contents/mods/AutoAll/42/media/lua/client/`.

## Themes

Six things account for most of the list. Fix the theme, not the instances.

1. **Dismantle's fixes were never ported to its siblings.** Open, Sterilize,
   Tailoring, Read, VHS, Medicine and Cook each still carry two or more of:
   an in-transit item read as gone, bag contents counted twice, a plan going
   stale when gathering walks the character, a drained queue taken as proof
   a transfer landed, a job-ending counter that is cumulative instead of
   consecutive, and a stop message asserting a reason the code never
   established. Roughly 25 findings are these five bugs in different files.
   The right fix is a shared batch-job helper in Core so it cannot drift
   again, not a seventh hand-port.
2. **The safety model has holes at its edges.** Wringing undresses you and a
   stop leaves you that way. Rip in single player has no weight bound on its
   craft batch. A safety stop in Mechanics skips the closing sweep. The
   health-floor stop in Core ignores both the option that governs it and the
   hook Auto Medicine relies on.
3. **MuscleManager is a separate mod grafted on.** It has its own task
   registry, its own safety options, no ESC handling, and forces game speed
   every tick. Five highs. Either bring it inside the Auto All task model
   or ship it as the separate mod the Exercise gate already assumes exists.
4. **Tailoring does not work as a bulk tool.** Every patch in a batch is
   queued against one fabric instance, so only the first ever lands, and
   Train Tailoring unpicks patches you made before the job.
5. **Ownership is not tracked where it matters.** Mechanics will bolt your
   good spare onto the training wreck. Rip's corpse guard misses garments in
   a bag the corpse is wearing.
6. **The fork is not under version control.** Every fix since the first
   upload exists on this disk and on Steam, nowhere else.
