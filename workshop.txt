description=[h1]What is better[/h1]
description=
description=[h2]Corpse piles actually work now[/h2]
description=Standing on a pile of zombies and dismantling their watches used to fail in three separate ways. All three are fixed.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]No more "those are being worn or carried".[/b] The game answers the question "is this item equipped" on behalf of whoever owns the container, and for a watch lying on a body that is the corpse. Every item on the pile therefore claimed to be worn by [i]you[/i]. Auto Rip already knew this. Auto Dismantle did not.
description=[*][b]No more stopping halfway through a pile.[/b] One corpse-worn watch used to make every other watch of that type invisible, including ones already in your bag. The dismantle recipe refuses worn items, so the engine answered "no recipe" for the one on the body, and that answer got cached against the item type. That is why a batch would do all the red watches and then stop dead when they turned blue.
description=[*][b]Bodies are stripped first.[/b] A watch cannot be taken apart while a corpse is wearing it, so there is now a looting phase that takes them off first, using the same batch sizes and messages Auto Rip already uses. Previously there was no way to reach them at all.
description=[/list]
description=Two smaller things came out of the same work: the menu entry is no longer greyed out when everything in reach is still on a body, and a corpse is never used as the destination for the scrap.
description=
description=[h2]Auto Mechanics stops hauling wheels around[/h2]
description=If you have trained mechanics and watched your character carry four tyres across the car park while their health drains, this is that.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Parts are no longer carried for the whole job.[/b] An install used to wait while anything it blocks had an unfinished cycle, and unfinished is not the same as workable. A brake under the success floor, missing its tool, out of reach or out of retries is unfinished forever, so the tyre in front of it waited forever.
description=[*][b]The lap is a clean anti-clockwise loop again.[/b] The working order was always correct and always being followed. What broke it was corners never finishing, so the character walked away still holding the tyre and the installs landed later in a second lap. Fixing the wait fixed the route.
description=[*][b]A part that genuinely has to wait goes on the ground[/b] where it came off, and is picked back up when its install comes due, instead of riding along in your arms.
description=[/list]
description=Vanilla and modded vehicles alike. The cause was in the mod, not in any car.
description=
description=[h2]Counts you can trust[/h2]
description=The "N nearby" figure came out at [b]double[/b]. The inventory sweep already walks your worn bags, and the container list then handed those same bags back, so anything in a bag was counted twice. The menu would offer 12 and the job would take 6 apart. Auto Rip counted the same way and is fixed with it.
description=
description=[h2]Jobs that stop now tell you why[/h2]
description=Both Auto Dismantle and Auto Rip used to answer every dead end with the same message. "No screwdriver within reach" was shown to players holding a screwdriver, and "something went wrong" was shown for anything else.
description=[list]
description=[*]Each job now distinguishes [b]nothing here[/b], [b]no tool[/b] and [b]nothing workable right now[/b], and says which one it hit.
description=[*][b]Items in shelves and crates no longer refuse to work.[/b] The batch is planned before the fetching runs, and fetching walks your character, which changes what counts as within reach. A plan that goes stale that way is now re-planned instead of being reported as a pile that cannot be worked.
description=[*][b]A loot round that moved nothing says so[/b] instead of quietly planning another identical round until the budget runs out. On a multiplayer client a transfer can be discarded silently, and a drained queue was being treated as proof it happened.
description=[*]Every dead end also writes one line to console.txt with the numbers the planner decided from, so a report can be acted on instead of guessed at.
description=[/list]
