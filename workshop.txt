description=
description=[h2]🏠 Everyday chores[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]🍳 [b]Auto Cook[/b] - Builds a full meal on its own: finds the ingredients within reach, spreads the food types so the dish does not depress your character, seasons it and puts the leftovers back where they came from. Aim for calories, for hunger, or for a balance of the two. Rotten, burnt, poisonous and favourite items are left alone, and a window shows you exactly what is going in - cap any ingredient, or drop it from the pot, before it starts. An empty pot is fine: with a sink, barrel or well within four tiles, your character fills it first.
description=[*]🩹 [b]Auto Medicine[/b] - Right click any body part in the Health window and choose [b]Treat All Wounds[/b]. Bullets and glass come out, burns are washed, fractures are splinted, wounds are disinfected and bandaged - every injured part, in one go. It reaches for a sterilised bandage first when you have one, because that is what the game counts as a disinfected dressing. Optional poultices are off by default and skipped while bleeding.
description=[*]🧹 [b]Auto Clean[/b] - Washes you, your clothes (worn or stored), bloody weapons and dirty bandages. It finds the water and the soap, wrings each garment out and dresses you again afterwards.
description=[*]🧼 [b]Auto Sterilize[/b] - Sterilises bandages and rags in batches with the alcohol or boiled water within reach, and puts the bottle back at the end.
description=[*]🥫 [b]Auto Open[/b] - Opens tins, jars, ammo cartons and boxes within reach. Also packs ammo into boxes and matching boxes into cartons. Results can return to their source.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]🎒 Gear and salvage[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]✂️ [b]Auto Rip[/b] - Turns a whole wardrobe into sheets and strips in one go, corpses included: it strips the bodies first, then rips. Never touches worn, held or favourite items.
description=[*]🪡 [b]Auto Repair[/b] - Finds and sews the holes in one garment, or in everything within reach at once, choosing suitable fabric.
description=[*]🔌 [b]Auto Dismantle[/b] - Takes radios, walkie-talkies, TVs, torches, watches and cameras apart for Electrical XP. It fetches them out of containers, picks the screwdriver up only once, and can send the scrap straight back to the crate it came from.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]📚 Learning and training[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]📖 [b]Auto Read[/b] - Reads every skill book and recipe magazine within reach that still teaches you something, one after the other, and puts each one back. Books above or below your level are skipped.
description=[*]📼 [b]Auto VHS[/b] - Right click a tape and your character plays every recording within reach that still teaches a skill, through the nearest working video player, taking each one out when its programme ends. Tapes that only pass the time are left alone.
description=[*]💪 [b]Auto Exercise[/b] - Tick AUTO on the vanilla Fitness panel and your character trains, rests when endurance runs out, sits down or lies on a bed nearby to recover faster, takes something for the pain, eats when hunger kicks in, and starts the next set on its own. A summary card shows the sets, the time and the XP when the session ends.
description=[*]🧵 [b]Train Tailoring[/b] - Patches and unpicks in a loop for Tailoring XP, on the material you choose.
description=[*]🔧 [b]Train Mechanics[/b] - Works round a vehicle part by part for Mechanics XP. It fetches the tools, opens the bonnet, refits everything it removes and leaves nothing in your pockets - and you set the minimum success chance, so it skips the parts the game would likely break instead of risking your car.
description=[*]🔫 [b]Train Reload[/b] - Loads and unloads pistols, revolvers, shotguns, rifles and magazines for Reloading XP.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]🎛️ Only the automations you actually want[/h2]
description=
description=Fourteen modules is a lot of right-click menu. Every one has its own switch, so the menu only ever shows what you use.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]A new [b]Auto All[/b] tab in your character window, between Skills and Health.
description=[*]Tick boxes in Mod Options, plus an [b]Advanced Options[/b] window you can move and resize without leaving the game.
description=[*]Sandbox options, so a server can switch a module off for everyone.
description=[*]Safety rules you set once: stop on movement, on aiming, on ESC, on damage, or as soon as a zombie is spotted.
description=[*]Every entry carries its own icon in the context menu.
description=[/list]
description=
description=Everything is on by default - nothing changes unless you want it to.

description=[*][b]Your parts stay yours.[/b] Auto Mechanics keeps hold of a part it put on the ground, and never dresses a wound it lacks the tool for.
