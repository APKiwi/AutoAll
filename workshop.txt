description=[h1]Auto All (Fixed)[/h1]
description=A fork of [b]Auto All[/b] by [b]Robson Foster[/b]. Everything the mod does is theirs. This fork started as a handful of multiplayer fixes, all of which went upstream with credit. It has since gone its own way: a full audit of every module with the findings fixed, and new features on top.

description=[b]Do not run this and the original Auto All at the same time.[/b] This fork declares the original as incompatible, so the mod list will stop you enabling both. Your settings carry over unchanged, the option names did not move.

description=[h1]New in this fork[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Auto Cook gets its own water.[/b] Right-click an empty pot, or open the setup window, and Soup and Stew are offered when a sink, barrel or well with enough water is within 4 tiles. The character walks over, fills the pot, then cooks. Tickbox in the cook options. Clean water is preferred, tainted only as a fallback, and the finished dish is never tainted.
description=[*][b]Auto Cook only takes what you could reach.[/b] The sweep is two tiles, never through a wall or a locked crate. It used to pull food through walls.
description=[*][b]Auto Exercise is a real Auto All job.[/b] ESC, stop-on-zombie and stop-on-damage apply to it, starting another job ends it cleanly, and everything it borrowed goes back. Game speed is latched instead of forced: change it yourself and the mod backs off.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]The audit pass[/h1]
description=Every module was read line by line against multiplayer behaviour. 86 findings, all fixed. The ones you would notice:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Stalls and false stops.[/b] The stall counter is consecutive, not cumulative, so one slow craft no longer adds up to a stop. Client batches only grow once the server confirmed the last round. Items still in transit are counted as in transit, not gone, so the first round of a job is no longer thrown away.
description=[*][b]Honest messages.[/b] Every job says why it stopped, and writes one line to console.txt with the numbers it decided from. Missing tool is reported as missing tool, not "something went wrong".
description=[*][b]Corpses.[/b] The corpse check walks the whole container chain, so an item in a bag on a body is not read as worn. Worn bags are no longer counted twice.
description=[*][b]Mechanics.[/b] A part put on the ground stays yours and goes back on the same car. Refits the part this job took off, not the best one in your bag. Stays at the corner while your part is on the ground there. Names any part left behind when the job ends. Puts the car down on a safety stop.
description=[*][b]Medicine.[/b] Never dresses a bullet or burn wound without the tool, never uses a dirty bandage, and says so. Each step runs a tick after its fetch, so the item is actually there.
description=[*][b]Cook.[/b] Skip rotten always beats the skill slider. Seasonings no longer count as a pleasant option. Disorganized cooks skip the return phase instead of failing it.
description=[*][b]Tailoring, Read, VHS.[/b] One fabric instance per patch, unpicks only its own. No worse-material fallback. A stuck book is skipped, not the job. A tape is credited only when it ran to the end.
description=[*][b]Clean, Reload, Sterilize, Open, Dismantle.[/b] Clean re-dresses on every stop and verifies it landed. Reload holds the refill until the queue is empty on a client. The batch jobs re-plan empty rounds instead of spinning.
description=[/list]
